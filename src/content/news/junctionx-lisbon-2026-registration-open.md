Técnico announced that registrations for JunctionX Lisbon 2026 were open until 7 October 2026 at 23:59. The international technology and AI hackathon was set for 10–11 October 2026 at Técnico's Oeiras campus.

## Key details

- Open to students from any university and of any nationality, in teams of one to five. No previous hackathon experience was required.
- Free to take part, with food, drinks and a rest area provided.
- Around 150 participants were to be selected through an application process.
- The winning team would earn a place at Junction 2026 in Espoo, Finland (13–15 November 2026), representing its university.
- Challenge partners: NOS, Sensei, Inspiring Solutions and Albatroz Engenharia. Technology community partners: Jane Street, Lovable, n8n and SSOJet.

The full programme, challenges, partners and results are preserved in the [JunctionX Lisbon 2026 archive](/past-events/2026/).
