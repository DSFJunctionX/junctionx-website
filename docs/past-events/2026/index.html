Winners

The winners

1st place overall · Inspiring Solutions challenge

No Other Choice

Team
David Santos, João Teodósio, Miguel Rodrigues, Tomás Lopes, Pedro SilvaFour students from Técnico’s Department of Computer Science and Engineering (DEI) and one from the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Lisbon (FCUL)
Prize
€1,500 and a place at Junction 2026 in Espoo, Finland

The team tackled Inspiring Solutions’ QuantumTrace challenge, which asked participants to uncover cryptography that quantum computing will make vulnerable. Their solution, which DEI refers to by the challenge’s name, combined a platform that checks source code, network traffic, cloud configurations, certificates and websites; a browser extension that flags insecure website connections; and a Visual Studio Code extension that suggests AI-assisted fixes.
