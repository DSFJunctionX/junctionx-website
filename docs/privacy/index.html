Privacy policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by the JunctionX Lisbon organising team. If you have any question about privacy or your personal data, contact us at lisbon@hackjunction.com.

What this website collects

This is a static website. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or our own cookies, and the site has no forms or user accounts. We do not collect personal information through it.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver pages, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Third-party content

The JunctionX Lisbon 2026 archive embeds a Google Maps map of the venue and loads a typeface from Google Fonts. When you open that page, your browser connects to Google, which may receive your IP address and set cookies in line with Google's privacy policy.

Links to external services, such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Discord and the Junction platform (hackjunction.app), lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

Contact

For questions or requests about your data, email lisbon@hackjunction.com.
