01 About

More than a weekend.

JunctionX Lisbon is the Lisbon home of JunctionX, the international hackathon series by Junction. Since its founding edition in 2025, it has brought together students, developers, researchers and companies to work on real challenges and turn ideas into working prototypes.

Each hackathon is a chapter in a longer story. This website keeps that story in one place: what is happening now, and a permanent record of past editions, with their challenges, partners, people and projects.

02 Community

Who it brings together

JunctionX Lisbon is for people who like to make things, whatever their background.

  • Students

    From any university or field of study, learning by building alongside others.

  • Developers

    Engineers and designers who want to prototype quickly and try new tools.

  • Researchers

    Academics who want to see ideas tested against concrete, real-world problems.

  • Mentors

    Experienced practitioners who guide teams through technical and product decisions.

  • Partners

    Companies and organisations that bring real challenges, expertise and support.

Want to take part, mentor, partner or help organise a future edition? Write to lisbon@hackjunction.com.
